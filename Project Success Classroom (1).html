    04 / Evidence trail

    Sources used in this learning site

    The site is limited to the three key PDFs and the historical source details you supplied. No external sources have been added.

    Pinto & Slevin (1987)

    Critical Factors in Successful Project Implementation. Used for the ten critical success factors and the process-oriented implementation model.

    Zwikael & Smyrk (2019)

    Project Management: A Benefit Realisation Approach. Used for outputs, utilisation, target outcomes, benefits realization, and the distinctions between management, ownership and investment success.

    Huemann & Turner (2024)

    The Handbook of Project Management material supplied. Used for the historical overview of success criteria, stakeholders, time horizons, business success, benefits/value and sustainability.

    Historical-source transparency: Barnes (1969), de Wit (1988), Atkinson (1999), Shenhar & Dvir (2007/2014), and Müller (2010) are not treated here as independently read original publications. Where included, their contributions are limited to what is reported in the supplied image and/or Huemann & Turner (2024). Müller is included only at the level stated in your table.
